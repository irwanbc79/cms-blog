## Editorial release gate

An article may be released only when it has:

1. A title and angle that are not a repeated template.
2. Verifiable primary sources for regulatory or technical claims.
3. Original operational value: calculation, checklist, decision table, case
   evidence, or first-hand process detail.
4. No guarantee of customs clearance, permits, tariff treatment, inspection,
   release time, project ROI, or commercial outcome.
5. A clear author/reviewer identity and current review date.
6. No substantial overlap with another portfolio site; use a unique teaser and
   canonical destination when subjects overlap.
